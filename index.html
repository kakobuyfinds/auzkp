The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list that may organize item links, categories, prices, sizes and shopping notes. Use it to build a shortlist, then verify the original listing. A spreadsheet entry does not establish availability, authenticity or quality. This page provides a category-based guide and links to CAFANS rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

05 / Answers before checkout

A little clarity goes a long way.

Common questions about Kakobuy spreadsheet finds, product links and shopping checks.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list that may organize item links, categories, prices, sizes and shopping notes. A spreadsheet entry is a starting point for research, not proof of availability, authenticity or quality.

Is this the official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide that links to CAFANS. It does not represent Kakobuy or the brands mentioned.

Can every CAFANS product link be used with Kakobuy?

Compatibility is not guaranteed. Check the original seller URL and the purchasing service's current supported links and item restrictions before ordering.

What should I check before buying shoes or clothing?

Check the exact variant, seller size chart, item measurements, materials, available photos, return conditions and total expected cost. Brand names and listing photos alone do not establish authenticity.

Does the listed item price include shipping?

Do not assume it does. Depending on the seller, service and destination, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes or duties may be separate.
